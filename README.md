**Physick** is an open-world island game built with [three.js](https://threejs.org) and [Rapier](https://rapier.rs). You start in the city of Verian City with $150 and a dinghy at the marina. Take what isn't nailed down, sell it at the pawn shop, buy faster boats, and try not to get seen doing it. Further out there's a navy destroyer and a fighter base.

## Title screen

You stand on the Verian waterfront by the marina while the city goes to pieces behind you: a getaway car with a squad car on its tail tearing up the street, robbers sprinting down the promenade with a cop chasing them, a burning wreck, cars going up in fireballs, a shootout down a side street, and a police chopper circling overhead. The menu music plays once you click anywhere (browsers won't play sound before you've clicked); **Click to play** starts the game.

## Story

Three weeks ago a bomb went off at Verian Central. One of the eleven dead was **Wren Takavic**, your closest friend. You were standing beside Wren and lost your right arm; you get around with a robotic one now. A cell called **the Ashen Hand** did it, and they're still in the city. The story opens the first time you press Play.

**Your phone** (`J`) holds your jobs (start or replay a mission), texts from Vic (each new job arrives as a message) and your key items. Calls come in on it mid-mission: press `J` to answer.

- **1 · Seed Money:** Wren's cousin Vic wants money before he'll find you names. Take Wren's old pistol, rob the Mini Mart's till (`R` at the counter with the gun out), then lose the cops and get clear of the shop.
- **2 · Sweet Tooth:** Scoops Ice Cream, by the plaza, has been shuttered since the bombing. Force the shutter (`E`) and go inside. Three waves of costumed ice cream mascots come out of the back, including the Big Scoop at the end. Then take the strange key hidden behind the counter.
- **3 · Down the Rabbit Hole:** the key is stamped with a pine tree. Take it to the old bunker off the Pine Ridge trail in Cherry Estate and unlock the door. Stairs lead down to an abandoned **underground lab** laid out as a maze: grimy tiled rooms, specimen tanks, gurneys and workbenches, almost pitch dark apart from a few flickering strip lights. You can't hear anything from up top, only the lab's own music. A **blood trail** leads through the maze to the two documents (a shipping manifest and a torn blueprint) on desks in its dead ends. Then climb back out.
- **4 · Dead Cargo:** the blueprint is a bomb, and its target is Verian Central Square, again. The parts came in on the **MV Halvard**, a container ship tied up off Saltmarsh's west pier. Climb the ladder aboard, fight off the Ashen Hand gunmen, and throw all six bomb parts (marked red on the map) over the side (`RMB` to throw). They're too awkward to bag. When the last one goes in, Vic calls: hold out for three minutes until his helicopter arrives over the bow pad, then grab the ladder to get lifted back to Verian City. The hold-out gets harder the longer it goes: reinforcements come faster and from more directions (up from below decks, up the side from the pier, over the bow and stern rails), and they shoot straighter. There are SMG gunners, armoured heavies with shotguns who push right in, and grenadiers who lob grenades at you (move!). There's a final push with 40 seconds left. Vic's friend drops body armour by the pad when the hold-out starts. Plays *Chase on Foot*.
- **5 · El Monstruo** (the last mission of Act I): Rafe Doucet, the Courier, is moving to the Hollows and you're his new driver. Get into **El Monstruo**, a green monster truck with flame decals, outside Dockside Motors by Saltmarsh's fishing dock. The Courier and his two men climb into the cab. Drive them to the Hollows and **don't touch him** while his men are aboard: press `G` too early and they kill you. At the drop-off his men climb down and walk off. Once they're gone, `G` deals with the Courier. Then race back to the new **Verian Scrap & Salvage** yard on the Verian docks (east waterfront, at the end of the southernmost street) while Ashen Hand cars and helicopters come after you. Ram the cars to pieces with El Monstruo (fourteen in all, each marked with a floating red arrow and a red dot on the minimap and map; armoured SUVs take two good hits, and they ram hard). El Monstruo is bulletproof: gunfire from the cars, choppers and police just sparks off it, so the danger is getting rammed; the choppers circle and shoot, then drop low across the road to block you, so drive straight into them. Once every car is wrecked, park the truck in the crusher and watch it get flattened. **Act I complete.** Plays the *Pursuer* theme.
- **Key items** (the key and the documents) are listed on the phone and at the bottom of the bag (`Tab`). They can't be sold, dropped or confiscated. The documents can't be used yet.
- Dying or getting busted fails a mission. You can retry or replay it from the phone.

## The world

- **Verian City** (where you start): a compact 5 × 5 block downtown with kerbed pavements, brick walk-ups with shopfronts and fire escapes, apartment blocks with balconies and glass towers on stone podiums. Plenty of ground floors are walk-in: shops with shelves of stuff, diners, office and police/hospital lobbies, and the **pawn shop** (sell anything at its counter with `E`). Cars drive the streets (they queue, turn, stop for people and honk at you) and pedestrians walk the pavements and cross at the corners. There's a plaza with a fountain, a pocket park, the **boat dealer** at the marina, a police station and a hospital.
- **Verian International Airport** (south-west of the city, over a causeway): closed. The gate's chained shut, the runway is coned off and marked with big yellow crosses, and three airliners sit parked on the apron.
- **Cherry Estate National Park** (behind Verian City, to the west): forested mountains, with peaks over 80 m out west. A road climbs from the city's west side up through the hills to the **Saltmarsh Gate Bridge**, an international-orange suspension bridge (two towers, sweeping cables, a 940 m deck 30 m above the water) that crosses the strait to Saltmarsh Key. Off the road: dirt trails over the hills, a ranger station at the trailhead, three campsites with tents and picnic tables (and stuff to steal), boulders, fallen logs, and a lookout tower on a hilltop. Hikers and a ranger wander the trails.
- **The Hollows** (east of Saltmarsh Key, over a short road bridge): the rough part of town. Cracked streets with potholes, boarded-up walk-ups and graffiti, two grim project towers, a liquor store and laundromat, dumped couches and burned-out cars. Two crews hold turf here: the **Eastside Kings** (red) at the basketball court and the **Hollow Boys** (green) in the burned-out lot. Act suspicious around them — get in their faces, come round waving a gun, speed at them, start trouble or drag a body past — and the "?" over their heads turns to "!": they come for you and the whole neighbourhood erupts in a **gang war**, with the crews pushing into each other's turf and shooting it out. It calms down once you've been gone a while. Gang members carry cash and sometimes a pistol.
- **Saltmarsh Key** (north of the city): a leafy suburb. Asphalt streets with kerbs, grass verges, footpaths, street trees and corner street lights, lined with family homes: one- and two-storey houses in lap siding with pitched shingle roofs, porches, shuttered windows, attached garages, driveways (some with a car in them), mailboxes, hedges and fenced back yards. In the middle is a market square full of stalls, with a church, a lighthouse, a general store and a fishing dock round the edge. Lots of stuff lying around on porches and stalls, and townspeople with cash in their pockets. The container ship **MV Halvard** is tied up off its west pier.
- **NAS Halyard** (east): the naval air station with the destroyer moored alongside and fighter jets on the apron.

## Money, the bag and boats

- Look at an item and press `B` to drop it straight in your **bag** (or press `B` while holding something). `Tab` opens the bag: take things out to hold, or drop them. The bag holds 40 kg.
- Sell at the **pawn shop**; cash bundles go straight into your pocket.
- Buy boats at **Boat sales** on the marina: Fishing Skiff, Speedboat, Patrol RIB, Cabin Cruiser and the Super Yacht. Bought boats wait at the marina berths; a sunk boat is back at the marina after a while.
- Boats: walk up to yours and press `E`; `W` / `S` throttle, `A` / `D` steer, `E` to get off at a pier or the coast. Ramming does damage both ways (tougher boats come off better).
- Your money, bag and boats are saved in your browser.

## Cars, guns and the back alleys

- **Smash the scenery:** trees and street lamps snap off and go flying when a car hits them at speed. Your car loses some speed and takes a little damage (El Monstruo barely notices). They're quietly put back a few minutes later when you're well away. Cars never spawn inside buildings, walls, trees or lamps.
- **Bailing out:** press `E` in a moving car to dive out: you go flying as a ragdoll in third person (the car keeps rolling) and take damage at high speed. Once you stop tumbling, press `Space` to get up. **Dying** on land works the same way: you crumple as a ragdoll in third person until you come round at the hospital. Move the mouse to look around your body.
- **Grand theft auto:** walk up to any car in traffic and press `E` to drag the driver out and take it, or smash your way into a parked one (police cars included). `W` / `S` drive and reverse, `A` / `D` steer, `Space` handbrake, `H` horn, `E` to get out. The driver runs off and calls the police; a cop car is a much bigger deal. Cars are physical: they shove each other, spin out, bounce off walls and push loose stuff around. Hits dent the bodywork and smash the glass; a beaten-up car smokes, then catches fire, then blows up. Drive off the edge and it sinks. You can run people over (that's murder if anyone sees).
- **The alleys:** three blocks have a grimy service alley through the middle, and each has someone in it:
  - **Vic** (arms dealer): pistol, shotgun, SMG, ammo, grenades and body armour.
  - **Rosa** (the fence): buys your whole bag for 30% more than the pawn shop, makes the heat go away for $500 a star, and sells a police scanner (every cop on your minimap; you lose them faster).
  - **Dimitri's chop shop:** park a stolen car in his alley and press `E` for cash. Cop cars pay best.
- **Guns:** hold one and `LMB` fires (hold for the SMG); `RMB` throws it. Ammo is per weapon type and shown next to the gun. Gunfire sends everyone running and counts as a crime if anyone sees. **Grenades:** `RMB` to throw, then run. **Armour:** hold it and press `F` to put it on; it soaks up most damage until it's shot through.
- **Chase music:** *Chase on Foot* plays at one or two stars, *Starvation Pursuer* takes over at three or more (toggle in Settings).

## The bank

- **Verian National Bank** faces the plaza from the south. Talk to a teller (`E`) to deposit or withdraw. Money in the bank is safe; money in your pocket is what you lose when you die (10%) or get busted (25%).
- **ATMs** stand on pavements around the city, outside the bank, at the marina, in Saltmarsh market and at the park's ranger station ($5 per withdrawal). Shoot or smash one open and it spills cash bundles (bag them with `B`).
- **Robbing the bank:** hold a gun at the counter and press `R`. The silent alarm goes off at once (three stars). Get round the end of the counter into the vault and stay there to fill the bag (around $14,000), then get out — that's four stars. The vault needs about ten minutes to refill.

## Shops, clerks and hostages

- **Every shop, diner and front desk is staffed.** Walk up to the counter and press `E` to buy (snacks and drinks heal you), pay for what you've picked up, see the doctor at the hospital, pay off fines at the police station, or sell at the pawn shop.
- **Shelves are stocked with what that shop sells:** groceries in the corner store, bottles in the liquor store, phones, consoles and laptops in the electronics shop, tools in the hardware store, pills and medkits in the pharmacy. Pick anything up and, if the clerk can see you, they'll tell you what you owe. Walk out without paying and it's shoplifting: the clerk gets angry, calls it in and won't serve you for a while. Some clerks (always in the liquor store and the pawn shop) keep a gun under the counter and will use it.
- **Hold-ups:** with a gun out at the counter, press `R` to empty the till. The clerk puts their hands up and the police are on the way.
- **Hostages:** look at a clerk or any passer-by up close and press `G` to grab them as a human shield. The police won't shoot or cuff you while you've got one; they keep their distance instead. You move slower, and `G` again lets them go (they run straight to the police).
- **Signs** all fit their text now, and the freestanding ones stand on posts.

## The map

Press `M` for the full map: every island, road, trail and landmark, the shops and dealers, ATMs, the police, and you. Scroll to zoom in on where you are.

## Crime and the police

- **Punch** (`LMB` with an empty hand) or swing a bat; **grab** someone (`RMB`) to knock them out. Kill someone and you can **search the body** (`E`) for cash and valuables.
- **Pick pockets:** get behind someone and hold `E`. If they notice, they run and call the police.
- **Witnesses:** anyone who sees a crime runs and phones it in. Townspeople who find **blood or a body** go to look, then call the police, and if you're standing around nearby they'll describe you. People also notice you dragging a body around. Mops (in the plaza, the market and on the fishing dock) clean blood off the street.
- **Wanted level** (1–5 stars): officers on foot, then squad cars that chase you down the streets and run you over, and at sea police boats that ram your boat. From 2 stars they hit back, from 3 they shoot. Stay out of sight and the stars drain away; flying a jet loses them.
- **Busted:** let an officer stay on you for a couple of seconds and you're arrested: fined a quarter of your cash and your bag confiscated. Die and you wake up at the hospital, minus the bill.

## Graphics settings

Open **Settings** from the pause screen. The **Low / Medium / High / Ultra** presets set everything at once, or adjust these yourself:

- **Resolution:** 50%, 75%, 100% or Native (full sharpness on high-DPI screens). This makes the biggest difference to speed.
- **Dynamic resolution:** drops the resolution automatically when the frame rate dips, then raises it again.
- **Shadows:** Off, Low, High or Ultra (shadow map size and how far shadows reach).
- **View distance:** Near, Medium or Far. Scenery hidden in the haze isn't drawn at all.
- **Crowds and traffic:** fewer or more people and cars.
- **Antialiasing:** applies the next time the game loads.
- **Frame rate cap:** 30 or 60 FPS, or off. **Show FPS:** on or off.

Shelf stock in the shops is only drawn when you're at the shop. Distant scenery chunks are skipped, and the warship isn't drawn when it's far off.

## Controls

**On foot**
- `W A S D` walk, `Shift` run, `Space` jump
- `LMB` pick up the item you're looking at (it's held in your robot-arm hand), `LMB` again to put it down; empty-handed, `LMB` punches
- `RMB` throw the held item; empty-handed, `RMB` grabs a person (knocking them out) and drags them
- `F` use the held item (medkits heal, coffee perks you up)
- `B` bag it, `Tab` open the bag
- `E` interact: shops, the bank and ATMs, boats, cars, searching bodies, picking pockets, jets, the ship's helm and guns
- `M` full map · `R` rob the bank or a shop's till (holding a gun, at the counter) · `G` take or release a hostage · `J` phone (jobs, messages, key items, calls)
- Look at a body and `LMB` to drag it; `LMB` lets go, `RMB` heaves it
- Holding a mop: hold `LMB` to scrub blood, `RMB` to put it down
- `Esc` pause (Settings are on the pause screen)

**In a jet** (walk up to one on the airfield and press `E`)
- `W` / `S` throttle, `Shift` afterburner (at full throttle)
- Mouse to pitch and roll (the stick re-centres when you stop moving the mouse), `A` / `D` rudder and nosewheel steering
- `B` (or `Space`) brakes, `G` landing gear, `LMB` cannon, `C` cockpit / chase view
- `E` to climb out once you've landed and stopped
- Take off: line up on the runway, full throttle (+ afterburner), and pull back gently once you pass ~115 kn. Land: gear down, about 135 kn, a gentle descent onto the runway, then brakes.

**On a gun**
- Mouse to train and elevate, `LMB` to fire (hold for the CIWS), `E` to step away
- The 5-inch gun forward fires shells on a ballistic arc; the two CIWS mounts on the 01 deck aft fire tracer streams over their own side and astern
- Sink the red-and-white target rafts floating around the ship

**At the helm**
- `W` / `S` move the engine order telegraph up / down (BACK 1/3, STOP, AHEAD 1/3, 2/3, FULL, FLANK)
- `A` / `D` put the rudder over to port / starboard (it eases back amidships when you let go)
- `H` sound the ship's horn
- `E` leave the helm

**Going ashore:** while the ship is moored there's an opening in the rail amidships on the side facing the quay. Walk down the gangway (or just jump off) and you're ashore; walk back up the gangway to come aboard. Sail away by ringing up the engines at the helm; to moor again, bring her back alongside the quay, slowly and roughly parallel, and ring STOP.

**Getting below:** the doorways in the middle of the superstructure, on both sides of the main deck.

**Getting to the bridge:** from the flight deck at the stern, walk forward along either side of the hangar, climb the stairs amidships to the 01 level, go forward between the funnels and the boats, and through the door at the back of the bridge.

## What's in it

- **Ocean:** Gerstner waves in a shader, with a matching CPU wave function so the ship heaves, pitches and rolls on the waves you see. Bow wave, Kelvin wake and churned water astern scale with speed.
- **The ship:** a procedural destroyer: lofted hull with hull number, walkable main deck, 5-inch gun, VLS, superstructure with stairs, two funnels, boats, CIWS, hangar and flight deck, a mast with spinning radar, and an enclosed bridge with consoles, wheel and engine telegraph. She heels into turns.
- **Interior:** doorways amidships on both sides open into a cross passage, with the crew mess forward (tables, galley, mugs and rations) and the storeroom aft (shelving racks and lockers stocked with ammo boxes, toolboxes, medkits, extinguishers, jerry cans, life jackets, rope, spare shells and more). Everything can be picked up, carried, re-shelved or thrown.
- **Your hand:** a robotic arm (you lost your own arm in the Verian Central bombing that killed Wren); whatever you pick up is shown clamped in its gripper.
- **Gunnery:** man the 5-inch gun or either CIWS; rounds fly in world space with gravity and the ship's own speed, splash into the sea and blow up target rafts.
- **Naval air station:** a quay with bollards and lamp posts, an apron with three hangars, a control tower, container stacks and fuel tanks, a 1.3 km runway with a parallel taxiway, and a windsock. It has its own physics world, so you can walk around it while the ship sails off.
- **Fighter jets:** procedural twin-tail navy fighters with a physical-ish flight model (thrust, drag, lift from angle of attack, stalls), taxiing, take-off, landing (or crashing), afterburners, a nose cannon that can hit the target rafts, flight HUD with flight-path marker and STALL / PULL UP warnings. Wrecked jets are replaced after a while.
- **Casualties:** sailors hit hard (a thrown item, cannon fire from a jet) are killed and go ragdoll. Blood sprays, pools under the body and smears when you drag it. Mops are in the stores, by the mess and on the flight deck. Loose bodies and items feel the ship's roll at sea.
- **Settings** (pause screen): townspeople respawn, chase music, crew respawn and crew size on the destroyer, blood on/off, bodies stay or get cleared, mouse sensitivity, invert jet pitch. Saved in your browser.
- **Crew:** sailors in working uniforms, cranials and dixie cups wander the main deck and stop to look at you when you walk up to them.
- **Health:** fall damage and going overboard hurt; health regenerates; dying sends you to the hospital in Verian City.

Physics runs in the ship's own frame (the ship is static there) and the whole frame is moved on the ocean for rendering, so walking on a rolling deck is solid.

## Running it

```bash
npm install
npm run dev      # http://localhost:5173
npm run build    # static build in dist/
```

Pushing to `main` deploys to GitHub Pages through the included workflow.
