**Physick** is an open-world island game built with [three.js](https://threejs.org) and [Rapier](https://rapier.rs). You start in the city of Verian City with $150 and a dinghy at the marina. Take what isn't nailed down, sell it at the pawn shop, buy faster boats, and try not to get seen doing it. Further out there's a navy destroyer and a fighter base.

## Title screen

Nothing loads until you click **Click to start**. That click also lets the browser play sound, so the menu music starts straight away while the city builds behind the title screen; a small loading popup in the corner shows progress and removes itself when it's done.

You stand on the Verian waterfront by the marina while the city goes to pieces behind you: a getaway car with a squad car on its tail, robbers sprinting down the promenade with a cop chasing them, a burning wreck, a side-street shootout and a police chopper circling. On a loop:
1. Two airliners on a collision course meet overhead in a fireball. Burning debris rains down on the city, and a flaming section of fuselage slams into the ground a few metres from you.
2. El Monstruo comes roaring down the waterfront and shoves the wreckage out of the way.
3. A swarm of police cars comes down the street, straight into El Monstruo coming the other way, which smashes every one of them.
4. Killer clown vans turn up and do burnouts in clouds of tyre smoke.

**Click to play** starts the game. Under it are **Multiplayer**, **Settings** and **Our other games**, which links to Tacocraft, Alchemo and God of Tree; each opens in a new tab. The same menu is on the pause screen.

## Story

Three weeks ago a bomb went off at Verian Central. One of the eleven dead was **Wren Takavic**, your closest friend. You were standing beside Wren and lost your right arm; you get around with a robotic one now. A cell called **the Ashen Hand** did it, and they're still in the city. The story opens the first time you press Play.

**Your phone** (`J`) holds your jobs (start or replay a mission), texts from Vic (each new job arrives as a message) and your key items. Calls come in on it mid-mission: press `J` to answer.

- **1 · Seed Money:** Wren's cousin Vic wants money before he'll find you names. Take Wren's old pistol, rob the Mini Mart's till (`R` at the counter with the gun out), then lose the cops and get clear of the shop.
- **2 · Sweet Tooth:** Scoops Ice Cream, by the plaza, has been shuttered since the bombing. Force the shutter (`E`) and go inside. Three waves of costumed ice cream mascots come out of the back, including the Big Scoop at the end. Then take the strange key hidden behind the counter.
- **3 · Down the Rabbit Hole:** the key is stamped with a pine tree. Take it to the old bunker off the Pine Ridge trail in Cherry Estate and unlock the door. Stairs lead down to an abandoned **underground lab** laid out as a maze: grimy tiled rooms, specimen tanks, gurneys and workbenches, almost pitch dark apart from a few flickering strip lights. You can't hear anything from up top, only the lab's own music. A **blood trail** leads through the maze to the two documents (a shipping manifest and a torn blueprint) on desks in its dead ends. Then climb back out.
- **4 · Dead Cargo:** the blueprint is a bomb, and its target is Verian Central Square, again. The parts came in on the **MV Halvard**, a container ship tied up off Saltmarsh's west pier. Climb the ladder aboard, fight off the Ashen Hand gunmen, and throw all six bomb parts (marked red on the map) over the side (`RMB` to throw). They're too awkward to bag. When the last one goes in, Vic calls: hold out for three minutes until his helicopter arrives over the bow pad, then grab the ladder to get lifted back to Verian City. The hold-out gets harder the longer it goes: reinforcements come faster and from more directions (up from below decks, up the side from the pier, over the bow and stern rails), and they shoot straighter. There are SMG gunners, armoured heavies with shotguns who push right in, and grenadiers who lob grenades at you (move!). There's a final push with 40 seconds left. Vic's friend drops body armour by the pad when the hold-out starts. Plays *Chase on Foot*.
- **5 · El Monstruo** (the last mission of Act I): Rafe Doucet, the Courier, is moving to the Hollows and you're his new driver. Get into **El Monstruo**, a green monster truck with flame decals, outside Dockside Motors by Saltmarsh's fishing dock. The Courier and his two men climb into the cab. Drive them to the Hollows and **don't touch him** while his men are aboard: press `G` too early and they kill you. At the drop-off his men climb down and walk off. Once they're gone, `G` deals with the Courier. Then race back to the new **Verian Scrap & Salvage** yard on the Verian docks (east waterfront, at the end of the southernmost street) while Ashen Hand cars and helicopters come after you. Ram the cars to pieces with El Monstruo (fourteen in all, each marked with a floating red arrow and a red dot on the minimap and map; armoured SUVs take two good hits, and they ram hard). El Monstruo is bulletproof: gunfire from the cars, choppers and police just sparks off it, so the danger is getting rammed; the choppers circle and shoot, then drop low across the road to block you, so drive straight into them. Once every car is wrecked, park the truck in the crusher and watch it get flattened. **Act I complete.** Plays the *Pursuer* theme.

**Act II: Fallout.** The papers from the lab lead to **the Chemist**, the Ashen Hand's chemical weapons man, who is cooking a nerve agent in a private hangar at the airport and plans to fly it out.

- **6 · Paper Trail:** take the papers to **Ines Moreau**, a forger outside the Liquor·Lotto in the Hollows. Then go to Doucet's lock-up, unit 7 at **Marsh Storage** (on the grass behind Saltmarsh's town row). The Ashen Hand got there first: deal with them, search his desk (a burner phone and an unmarked keycard). Then a black SUV of reinforcements roars in: fight them or get clear.
- **7 · The Clean-Up Crew:** Doucet's phone rings with a meeting at the old airliner wreck in the Scorch. It's an ambush. Fight it out until their boss runs for a black SUV, chase it across the salt flat (he leans out of the window and shoots back), wreck it, and search it for the airport's security schedule.
- **8 · The Airport Manager** (stealth): wait across the street from **Harlan Pike**'s city office, then tail his white car to the Verian docks without getting too close (under ~18 m for long) or losing him (over ~170 m). He meets two of the Ashen Hand at the scrapyard gate. Stay out of their sight and photograph Pike with one of them (Vic gives you a camera: take it out, hold `RMB` to zoom, which lets you shoot from much further away, and click). When they've gone, show Pike the photos. He hands over his all-areas pass, and you choose: **let him go** or **he doesn't walk away**. Nothing changes either way.
- **9 · Blueprints** (stealth): in the airport terminal, Pike's pass opens the **STAFF ONLY** door at the east end of the hall (`E` at the card reader, and it stays open). Behind it is the **Airport Authority** wing: get past three patrolling guards and two sweeping security cameras to the safe in Records (the far south-east room). Hold `E` for a few seconds to crack it, take the Hangar 3 plans, and get out. Being seen (a guard or camera's yellow cone fills up: `?` then `!`) or firing a shot fails it. Sneak up behind a guard who hasn't noticed you and press `E` for a **silent takedown**.
- **10 · Gate Crasher** (stealth, disguised): you're in the ground crew's hi-vis vest and hard hat. Crew don't look twice at crew, unless you run, get your gun out, have the police on you, or walk right up to them. Go through the staff wing and out of its back door onto the apron (that's the way airside), then at **Hangar 3** (the western hangar), stick trackers on the Chemist's jet and the fuel truck and read the departures board in the site office. Then walk back out through the staff wing into the terminal hall. Gunfire blows your cover. Silent takedowns work here too (if nobody's watching).
- **11 · Last Flight** (the last mission of Act II): the Chemist is taking off now. Climb aboard the empty **Verian Air airliner** at the east end of the runway, take off (it's heavy: full throttle and pull back past ~200 km/h), and catch his dark jet with the red hand on the tail (a red bracket on screen, or an arrow at the edge when he's off it). You can't shoot it down: Vic calls and tells you to ram it. Point the airliner straight at him until the bracket turns gold (**EJECT · F**) and **eject** (`F`). If you were lined up and close enough, the empty plane flies itself into him: slow motion, a fireball, and the agent burning off green. Open your parachute (`Space`; `A`/`D` steer, `W` dive, `S` flare) and land. **Act II complete.**
- **Checkpoints:** fail missions 8–11 and retry from the phone straight away, and you start back at the mission's checkpoint (across from Pike's office, by the staff door in the terminal, just airside, or by the airliner) instead of having to travel there again.
- **Stealth:** `C` crouches (slower, lower and much harder to see; running stands you up). Guards and cameras show their view as a cone on the floor that turns red as they notice you.
- **Key items** (the key, the documents, and Act II's phone, keycard, schedule, photos, pass, plans and flight plan) are listed on the phone and at the bottom of the bag (`Tab`). They can't be sold, dropped or confiscated.
- Dying or getting busted fails a mission. You can retry or replay it from the phone.

## The world

- **Verian City** (where you start): a compact 5 × 5 block downtown with kerbed pavements, brick walk-ups with shopfronts and fire escapes, apartment blocks with balconies and glass towers on stone podiums. Plenty of ground floors are walk-in: shops with shelves of stuff, diners, office and police/hospital lobbies, and the **pawn shop** (sell anything at its counter with `E`). Cars drive the streets (they queue, turn, stop for people and honk at you) and pedestrians walk the pavements and cross at the corners. There's a plaza with a fountain, a pocket park, the **boat dealer** at the marina, a police station and a hospital.
  - **Every building is a real building now.** Walk in the front door of any block and the whole thing is inside, with no dead space: a stairwell climbs every floor (switchback flights with a landing at each level) and towers of five floors or more have a **lift** too (press `E` at the lift doors and pick a floor). Apartment blocks have a corridor on each floor with flats off it: people live in them (they're home, pottering about their kitchens and sofas), some are empty, and the ones with a red **FOR RENT** sign on the door you can **rent** (see Homes). Brick walk-ups have a shop or diner on the ground floor and flats above; office towers have open-plan floors with desks, meeting rooms and staff. The stairs go all the way up to a **rooftop door**: every roof is walkable, with solid parapets, water tanks, AC units and aerials you can't fall through.
  - **The layout is fixed**: every street, building and shop is in the same place every time you play (and in multiplayer everyone has the same city). The landmarks sit where they make sense: **Verian Motors** on the west corner where the highway from the bridge comes into the city, and **MARCOS** on the south-east corner by the airport and Scorch causeways.
  - **MARCOS** is Verian's burger joint: red brick and big windows under a red roof, a tall pole sign with the burger logo you can see down both roads (lit up at night), a patio with umbrellas, a car park and a **drive-thru** lane down the east side. Inside are booths, a play corner with a ball pit and slide, the kitchen and the crew at the counter under the menu boards. Press `E` at the counter (or at the drive-thru window in a car) to order: the Marco Burger ($6, +30 health), Big Marco ($9, +50), Chicken Bites ($5, +25), Fries ($3, +12), a Choc Shake ($4, +15) or the Meal Deal ($12, full health). Start shooting in there and the crew run for it, and it's closed for a while.
- **Verian International Airport** (straight south of the city, over a short causeway from the end of 5th street): open. Walk into the **terminal**: three check-in islands with their airline boards, a café and duty free shop, rows of seats looking out at the airliners on the stands, departures boards, an information desk and the security lanes to the gates. It's busy with travellers wheeling suitcases who come in off the kerb, queue, sit, wander and leave (and scatter if a gun goes off). A Verian Air airliner takes off from runway 27 every few minutes, and another lands. Airside (the stands, the hangars, the runway) is fenced off: the only way through is the Airport Authority's staff wing, behind a door Pike's pass opens.
- **Cherry Estate National Park** (behind Verian City, to the west): forested mountains, with peaks over 80 m out west. A road climbs from the city's west side up through the hills to the **Saltmarsh Gate Bridge**, an international-orange suspension bridge (two towers, sweeping cables, a 940 m deck 30 m above the water) that crosses the strait to Saltmarsh Key. Traffic runs both ways the whole length of it, from Verian City up through the hills, over the bridge and down into Saltmarsh. Off the road: dirt trails over the hills, a ranger station at the trailhead, three campsites with tents and picnic tables (and stuff to steal), boulders, fallen logs, and a lookout tower on a hilltop. Hikers and a ranger wander the trails. Down in the gloom under the bridge's first span, a **dirt lane** that no traffic uses runs back towards Verian City; at its end is **Underpass Customs** (see below).
- **The Hollows** (Verian's north end, built right onto the top of the city: its avenues just carry on north under the **Welcome to the Hollows** arch, and a causeway runs across from 7th avenue to the tip of Pelican Point): the rough part of town, but alive. Cracked streets with potholes, walk-ups with black iron fire escapes and plywood over some windows, big painted murals, two grim project towers on a bare plaza, the Liquor·Lotto, a coin laundry and a 24/7 bodega, Tito's Boxing Gym and Ink & Iron Tattoo, the Suds car wash, the **Hollows Community Garden** (raised beds and sunflowers), a taco truck on an open lot under strung lights, more lights strung over a few streets, an open car park on the north shore, dumped couches and burned-out cars. Two crews hold turf here: the **Eastside Kings** (red) at the basketball court and the **Hollow Boys** (green) in the burned-out lot. Act suspicious around them — get in their faces, come round waving a gun, speed at them, start trouble or drag a body past — and the "?" over their heads turns to "!": they come for you and the whole neighbourhood erupts in a **gang war**, with the crews pushing into each other's turf and shooting it out. It calms down once you've been gone a while. Gang members carry cash and sometimes a pistol.
- **Saltmarsh Key** (north of the city): a leafy suburb. Asphalt streets with kerbs, grass verges, footpaths, street trees and corner street lights, lined with family homes: one- and two-storey houses in lap siding with pitched shingle roofs, porches, shuttered windows, attached garages, driveways (some with a car in them), mailboxes, hedges and fenced back yards. In the middle is a market square full of stalls, with a church, a lighthouse, a general store and a fishing dock round the edge. Lots of stuff lying around on porches and stalls, and townspeople with cash in their pockets. A strip of **beach** runs down the whole west shore, with umbrellas, towels, a lifeguard tower and a volleyball net, sloping gently into the shallows. The container ship **MV Halvard** is tied up off its west pier.
  - **The town row** along the north shore, facing the last street:

    | Building | What you can do there |
    |---|---|
    | **Saltmarsh Elementary School** | A playground out the back; a yellow school bus at the kerb |
    | **Fire Station No. 4** | Red engine-bay doors; a fire engine parked outside |
    | **Saltmarsh Police** | Walk in and pay your fines; a patrol car outside |
    | **Saltmarsh Hospital** | See the doctor; if you go down nearer here than Verian City, this is where you come round; an ambulance outside |
    | **Pharmacy** | Medicine and painkillers |
    | **Public Library** | Walk in |
    | **Saltmarsh Savings** | A bank branch |
    | **Town Hall** | Walk in; a flagpole outside |
    | **Marsha's Diner** | Food |
    | **Saltmarsh Threads** | The clothes shop (see below) |
    | **Saltmarsh Guns & Ammo** | The gun store |

    The fire engine, ambulance, school bus and patrol car can all be stolen.
- **NAS Halyard** (east): the naval air station with the destroyer moored alongside and fighter jets on the apron.
- **Pelican Point** (a long headland running south off the corner of Saltmarsh Key, pointing straight at Verian): drive off the end of Saltmarsh's town row and on down it, or come over the causeway from the Hollows. A fishing village on the headland: cottages round a village green, the Pelican Point Seafood shack, a jetty with fishing boats bobbing at it, a beach with umbrellas, and the red-and-white lighthouse up on the rocky tip facing the city (a dirt track runs up to it, and its beam sweeps the sea at night). The road loops right round it, and from the tip the causeway carries on to the Hollows: the land route from Saltmarsh into Verian.
- **Ridgeback Hills** (medium, north-west of Cherry Estate): take the coast road west from under the Saltmarsh Gate Bridge. Ridgeback Gas & Grocery at the foot (a proper modern station: canopy with lights, pump islands, a shop with a glass front, a price pylon), log cabins round a campfire in the meadow, a gravel quarry with terraced benches, heaps, a crusher and a dump truck, and the Ridgeback Pass climbing through crash-barriered hairpins to the observatory on the 120 m summit, with a viewpoint back over the city. Thick pine forest below the treeline.
- **The Scorch** (huge, south of the city, **Act II**): a desert as big as everything else put together, reached by the long causeway off the south end of Verian City. Until you finish Act I it's barricaded (ROAD CLOSED, and anyone who sneaks in by boat or air is turned back). Inside: sand dunes in the west, red mesas, the bone-white Bone Dry Salt Flat, Route 9 running the length of it, the Last Chance gas station (an old sun-bleached stop with round-topped pumps), the ghost town of Copper Gulch (saloon, sheriff, bank, hotel, water tower and windmill), an old airliner wreck, cacti, scrub and tumbleweeds.

## Things to do

- **Street races:** chequered flags mark five starts: **City Sprint** (2 laps round downtown, starting by Verian Motors), **Bridge Run** (point to point over the Saltmarsh Gate Bridge), **Hollows Dash** (2 laps), **Lighthouse Loop** (2 laps round Pelican Point, starting by the village green) and **Ridgeback Hillclimb** (point to point up the Ridgeback Pass, from the gas station to the observatory). The other drivers tuck in behind you rather than ram you, and brake for hairpins. Stop in any car on the start and press `E`: $500 to enter, and you can **bet on yourself** to win ($500 / $2,000 / $10,000 at 3 to 1). A countdown, then four other drivers and a string of checkpoints (the blue beacon is the next one). Prizes for the top three; your best times are kept. The music switches to *Time Trial* for the race.
- **Vehicle jobs:** press `Y` in a **taxi** (three in city traffic, one at the plaza rank), the **ambulance** or the **fire engine** (both outside the hospital) to go on duty, and `Y` again to clock off.
  - Taxi: pick up the fare waving at the kerb (stop next to them), drive them to where they're going. Faster pays more.
  - Paramedic: get to the patient on the pavement, then back to the hospital's front door.
  - Firefighter: get to the burning dumpster and hold `F` to spray the water cannon until it's out (it'll knock people over, too).
  Each job gets a time limit; keep going for bigger pay.
- **Odd jobs** on your phone (`J` → *Odd jobs*): a **drop-off** for Vic (a package across town against the clock), a **repo** for Dimitri (take the marked car to his chop shop: drive it in or tow it in), a **tail** for Rosa (follow the purple car for 90 seconds: not too close, don't lose it) and a **hot car** for Dimitri (a yellow Stiletto to the docks scrapyard; every scratch comes off your cut).
- **Stunt jumps:** four yellow-and-black ramps, each throwing you across a gap to somewhere else: over the strait between the Hollows and the tip of Pelican Point, beside the causeway (*Pelican plunge* north out of the Hollows' shore car park, *Point break* back south off the tip), and over the airport gate (*Gate crasher* in, *Jailbreak* out). Chevron boards line the run-up. Hit one at 80 km/h or more and the game drops into slow motion while you fly; the faster you go the further you fly. Stunt landings don't damage the car. $500 the first time each, $10,000 for all four; they're marked on the map.
- **Hidden packages:** thirty glowing green parcels hidden all over: kerb corners, alleys, piers, campsites, the airport, under the bridge. $100 each, and $5,000, $15,000 and $50,000 when you reach 10, 20 and all 30. Saved.

## Day, night, weather and radio

- **Day and night:** a full day takes 24 minutes (one game hour per minute; the clock's under the compass). Pink-orange sunrises and sunsets, moonlit nights full of stars, street lamps throwing pools of light, and every car with its headlights and tail lights on after dark.
- **Weather** changes every few hours: clear, cloudy, **rain** (slippery roads, the hiss of it), **storms** (lightning and thunder) and thick **fog**.
- **Car radio:** press `R` while driving to change station. *Verian FM 101.3*, *Pursuit 94.7* (the chase themes), *Static Lab 66.6*, and **Talk Verian 88.9**, where host Dale Marsh reads out the news (mostly what you've been up to: crimes, chases, SWAT call-outs, choppers shot down, houses and cars bought), takes useless calls and complains about the weather. The stations keep playing while you're tuned away. A chase theme drowns the radio out.
- **Around town:** people walk their dogs (on leads; if something happens to the owner, the dog bolts), joggers run the streets and trails, two **buskers** play in the plaza (press `E` to tip them $5; the sax player is a real person, so he can be shot, run over or knocked out like anyone else, and someone new takes his pitch a few minutes after you leave), and fishermen line the end of the Saltmarsh fishing dock.

## Money, the bag and boats

- Look at an item and press `B` to drop it straight in your **bag** (or press `B` while holding something). `Tab` opens the bag: take things out to hold, or drop them. The bag holds 40 kg.
- Sell at the **pawn shop**; cash bundles go straight into your pocket.
- Buy boats at **Boat sales** on the marina: Fishing Skiff, Speedboat, Patrol RIB, Cabin Cruiser and the Super Yacht. Bought boats wait at the marina berths; a sunk boat is back at the marina after a while.
- Boats: walk up to yours and press `E`; `W` / `S` throttle, `A` / `D` steer, `E` to get off at a pier or the coast. Ramming does damage both ways (tougher boats come off better).
- Your money, bag, boats and homes are saved in your browser.

## Homes

**Renting a flat:** the apartment doors with a red **FOR RENT** sign are for rent. Press `E` at the door to see the rent ($25 a day in the Hollows, $50 in town, $70 downtown) and take it: the first day is paid up front, then it's taken from your pocket or bank each morning. It's a real home: the key is yours, and once you sleep in the bed you wake up there when you load the game. Miss a payment and you're evicted. You can give up the lease at the door any time. Sleep in the bed to make it your home.

Houses with a red **FOR SALE** board out front are on the market; walk up and press `E` for details. You pay from your pocket first, then your bank account. Once you own a place it's **home**: you start there when you load the game, and after a bad day you come round there instead of at the hospital (no hospital bill). If you own more than one, pick which is home from its panel; you can also rest there to heal.

| Home | Where | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Walk-up in the Hollows | The Hollows | $4,500 |
| Saltmarsh bungalow | Saltmarsh Key | $85,000 |
| Saltmarsh family home | Saltmarsh Key | $160,000 |
| Plaza Tower penthouse | Verian City, by the plaza | $750,000 |

Every home has a furnished **inside**. Press `E` at the front door to go in (you can look round a place before buying it), and `E` at the door inside to go back out. Inside it's quiet: no sun, no street noise, just the lamps.

- **Hollows walk-up:** one room with green striped wallpaper, a single bed, a couch facing a telly on a milk crate, a kitchenette, a folding table with empties, and a view of the brick alley.
- **Saltmarsh bungalow:** an open-plan living room and kitchen, a dining table by the window, and a blue bedroom at the back.
- **Saltmarsh family home:** downstairs, a living room, a dining table for six and a big kitchen with an island; up the stairs, a master bedroom, a kid's room with a desk, toys and a bean bag, and a landing with a window seat.
- **Plaza Tower penthouse:** marble floors, a wall of glass onto the city skyline at dusk, a sectional sofa and a huge TV, a grand piano, a glass dining table for eight, a backlit bar, a designer kitchen, and a bedroom suite.

Press `E` at the bed in a home you own to sleep: you heal up, it becomes home, and you **choose when to wake up** (morning, midday, afternoon, evening, night, midnight, a few hours from now, or just a nap). The clock jumps on, the weather may have changed, your businesses keep taking money while you sleep and the stock market moves. You also wake up in that bed after a bad day. The view out of the windows follows the time of day.

To choose where you spawn, open the **Home** app on your phone (`J`): pick any home you own, or **Verian Plaza** to go back to the default (you start at the plaza and come round at the hospital).

### Life at home

Every home you own has a few extras inside (press `E` at them):

- **Wall safe:** stash cash. Money in the safe can't be lost to hospital bills, cops or muggers. It's one safe shared between all your homes.
- **Weapon locker:** put away guns, grenades, armour and ammo, and take them back out (or **Gear up** to grab the lot). Also shared between your homes.
- **TV:** VNN with the latest headlines (mostly about you), the stock market, and the weather.
- **Pool table** (family home and penthouse): eight-ball against Tony from next door, for fun or for a bet. Aim with the mouse, hold the button to build power, let go to shoot. Pot your group (spots or stripes) and then the black; pot the black early and you lose. The world waits while you play.

### Parking at home

Your cars from Verian Motors can live at your homes. Get out of one of your own cars near a home you own and it's parked there: next time you play it's waiting outside. You can also pick a home for each car at the Verian Motors desk (**Park at…**). The bungalow and family home have two spaces, the penthouse three (on the kerb outside the tower) and the Hollows one; if they're full, the car goes to the lot. A wrecked car's replacement is delivered to its home too.

## Cars, guns and the back alleys

- **Smash the scenery:** trees and street lamps snap off and go flying when a car hits them at speed. Your car loses some speed and takes a little damage (El Monstruo barely notices). They're quietly put back a few minutes later when you're well away. Cars never spawn inside buildings, walls, trees or lamps.
- **Bailing out:** press `E` in a moving car to dive out: you go flying as a ragdoll in third person (the car keeps rolling) and take damage at high speed. Once you stop tumbling, press `Space` to get up. **Dying** on land works the same way: you crumple as a ragdoll in third person until you come round at the hospital (or at home, if you own one). Move the mouse to look around your body.
- **Grand theft auto:** walk up to any car in traffic and press `E` to drag the driver out and take it, or smash your way into a parked one (police cars included). `W` / `S` drive and reverse, `A` / `D` steer, `Space` handbrake, `H` horn, `E` to get out. The driver runs off and calls the police; a cop car is a much bigger deal. Cars are physical: they shove each other, spin out, bounce off walls and push loose stuff around. They tilt with the road (nose up a hill, leaning on a camber), slow down climbing steep hills and pick up speed rolling down them, and have real gravity: fly off a crest, a ledge or a ramp at speed and you get a proper arc and a hard landing (no traction or steering in the air). Hits dent the bodywork and smash the glass; a beaten-up car smokes, then catches fire, then blows up. Drive off the edge and it sinks. You can run people over (that's murder if anyone sees).
- **The alleys:** three blocks have a grimy service alley through the middle, and each has someone in it:
  - **Vic** (arms dealer): pistol, shotgun, SMG, ammo, grenades and body armour.
  - **Rosa** (the fence): buys your whole bag for 30% more than the pawn shop, makes the heat go away for $500 a star, and sells a police scanner (every cop on your minimap; you lose them faster).
  - **Dimitri's chop shop:** park a stolen car in his alley and press `E` for cash. Cop cars pay best.
- **Saltmarsh Guns & Ammo** (on the town row along Saltmarsh's north shore, marked *Guns* on the map): a walk-in gun store with the good stuff. Prices include some rounds:

## Crime and the police

- **Punch** (`LMB` with an empty hand) or swing a bat; **grab** someone (`RMB`) to knock them out. Bare fists knock people out cold instead of killing them; it takes a weapon to kill. **Search** anyone lying on the ground, out cold or dead (`E`), for cash and valuables.
- **Out cold isn't safe:** someone lying unconscious can still be shot, kicked, run over or caught in an explosion, and that kills them. It counts as murder, unless they were a criminal.
- **Criminals** (gang members and the Ashen Hand's people, raiders and bounty hunters included): nobody calls the police when you shoot, beat, run over or kill one, or when you search their body. Knock one out and leave them: the nearest free officer in that area walks over and cuffs them (if there's nobody about, one is radioed in). If you're nearby and not wanted yourself, the officer tips you for the help: $60 for a gang member, $120 for an Ashen Hand thug, $200 for a bounty hunter. The gang still holds a grudge.
- **What people carry:** besides the usual wallets and phones:
  - **Shop clerks** have their shop's **keys** and something off their shelves, and armed clerks may have the shotgun from under the counter. With the keys (in your hand or bag), stand at that kind of shop's counter while nobody's behind it and press `E` to open the till quietly: no alarm, no witnesses. The till refills after five minutes.
  - **Bank tellers** carry a keycard; the police front desk has a badge and cuffs.
  - **Officers** carry a **badge**, sometimes **handcuffs** and a pistol; SWAT sometimes carry body armour or an SMG.
  - **Eastside Kings** wear **gold chains** and **Hollow Boys** **bandanas**. Either may carry **brass knuckles** (a melee weapon) or a pistol.
  - **The Ashen Hand** carry **signet rings** and **burner phones**, and bounty hunters a **bounty notice** with your face on it.
  - **The sax player** carries his **saxophone**, and the ice cream mascot a cone or two.

People also notice you dragging a body around. Mops (in the plaza, the market and on the fishing dock) clean blood off the street.

## Multiplayer

Up to **8 players** share one city: the same streets and shops, the same traffic and the same people on the pavements. Everyone keeps their own money, cars, homes and story.

- **Getting in:** on the title (or pause) screen, press **Multiplayer**, type your name, then **Create a room** (the server address is filled in already). You get a 4-letter room code; friends type it in and press **Join room**, or open the invite link (**Copy invite link**). The page reloads to build the room's city.
- **Who runs the city:** the first player in is the *host*. Their game drives the traffic and walks the pedestrians, and everyone else sees the same thing. Shoot someone, run them over or steal a car, and the host's game is told. If the host leaves or switches to another tab (browsers pause background tabs), the server hands the city to someone else. Their game carries on from what it was showing, and the clock follows the host's.
- **Other players:** you see them walking, driving, riding, boating and flying, with their name over their head and a pink dot on the map. You can shoot and punch each other, run each other over and catch each other in explosions. Get blown up, run down by someone's car, or caught by a good punch and you go flying as a ragdoll (everyone sees the same tumble; `Space` gets you back up), and so does a player who's killed. A kill is announced to everyone and pays the killer $250. Leave a car about and someone can steal it (bought cars are locked). The cops chasing a player are that player's, but everyone sees them, and anyone can shoot them.
- **Chat:** press **Enter**, type, then **Enter** again (**Esc** cancels). Joins, leaves, kills and car thefts show in the same feed.
- **Always a clear day:** in a room it's always early afternoon, with no rain, storms or fog, and the distance haze is pushed right back. Your single-player clock and weather aren't touched.
- **Not shared:** missions, gangs, shop clerks and the highway traffic run separately in each game. (The airport terminal's crowd is a single-player thing: in a room the terminal is quiet.) Inside homes and underground you're hidden from the others.

### Running the server

The game is a static site, so the multiplayer server runs separately. It's a small Node app in `server/` that hands out rooms and relays messages. It doesn't simulate anything, so it's cheap to run.

- **Render (free):** on [render.com](https://render.com), choose **New → Blueprint**, pick this repository, and it deploys from `render.yaml`. Your server address is the `https://…onrender.com` URL it gives you. Free servers go to sleep when nobody's playing, so the first room of the day takes up to a minute while it wakes up.
- **Anywhere else:** `cd server && npm install && npm start` (port `PORT`, default 8090), or use the `Dockerfile`. Put it behind HTTPS (the game is served over HTTPS, so it can only talk to a `https://` / `wss://` server).
- The game's default server is `https://physick-server.onrender.com` (set in `DEFAULT_SERVER` in `src/lobby.js`), so players don't need to type an address.

## Graphics settings

Open **Settings** from the pause screen. The **Low / Medium / High / Ultra** presets set everything at once, or adjust these yourself:

- **Resolution:** 50%, 75%, 100% or Native (full sharpness on high-DPI screens). This makes the biggest difference to speed.
- **Dynamic resolution:** drops the resolution automatically when the frame rate dips, then raises it again.
- **Shadows:** Off, Low, High or Ultra (shadow map size and how far shadows reach).
- **View distance:** Near, Medium or Far. Scenery hidden in the haze isn't drawn at all.
- **Crowds and traffic:** fewer or more people and cars.
- **Antialiasing:** applies the next time the game loads.
- **Frame rate cap:** 30 or 60 FPS, or off. **Show FPS:** on or off.

Shelf stock in the shops is only drawn when you're at the shop. Distant scenery chunks are skipped, and the warship isn't drawn when it's far off.

## Controls

**On foot**
- `W A S D` walk, `Shift` run, `Space` jump, `C` crouch, `X` ragdoll (go limp and keep your momentum: run or jump first for a real tumble; `X` or `Space` to get back up)
- `LMB` pick up the item you're looking at (it's held in your robot-arm hand), `LMB` again to put it down; empty-handed, `LMB` punches
- `RMB` throw the held item; empty-handed, `RMB` grabs a person (knocking them out) and drags them
- `F` use the held item (medkits heal, coffee perks you up)
- `B` bag it, `Tab` open the bag
- `E` interact: shops, the bank and ATMs, boats, cars, searching bodies, picking pockets, jets, the ship's helm and guns
- `M` full map · `R` rob the bank or a shop's till (holding a gun, at the counter) · `G` take or release a hostage · `J` phone (jobs, messages, key items, calls)
- Look at a body and `LMB` to drag it; `LMB` lets go, `RMB` heaves it
- Holding a mop: hold `LMB` to scrub blood, `RMB` to put it down
- `Esc` pause (Settings and Multiplayer are on the pause screen) · `Enter` chat (in a multiplayer room)

**In a jet** (walk up to one on the airfield and press `E`)
- `W` / `S` throttle, `Shift` afterburner (at full throttle)
- Mouse to pitch and roll (the stick re-centres when you stop moving the mouse), `A` / `D` rudder and nosewheel steering
- **Easy flying** (on by default; switch it off in Settings for the full flight model): mouse left/right turns and the plane banks itself, mouse up/down climbs and dives, let go and it rolls level. It lifts off by itself at full throttle, never stalls, won't let you fly into the ground or the sea, drops its gear when you're low and slow over land, and settles onto the ground in a gentle glide.
- `B` (or `Space`) brakes, `G` landing gear, `LMB` cannon, `C` cockpit / chase view, `F` eject (in the air: freefall, then `Space` for the parachute)
- `E` to climb out once you've landed and stopped
- Take off: line up on the runway, full throttle (+ afterburner), and pull back gently once you pass ~115 kn. Land: gear down, about 135 kn, a gentle descent onto the runway, then brakes.

**On a gun**
- Mouse to train and elevate, `LMB` to fire (hold for the CIWS), `E` to step away
- The 5-inch gun forward fires shells on a ballistic arc; the two CIWS mounts on the 01 deck aft fire tracer streams over their own side and astern
- Sink the red-and-white target rafts floating around the ship

**At the helm**
- `W` / `S` move the engine order telegraph up / down (BACK 1/3, STOP, AHEAD 1/3, 2/3, FULL, FLANK)
- `A` / `D` put the rudder over to port / starboard (it eases back amidships when you let go)
- `H` sound the ship's horn
- `E` leave the helm

**With a controller** (Xbox, PlayStation DualShock 4 / DualSense, or Nintendo Switch Pro; plug it in or pair it, then press any button)
- Buttons go by position, so on a Switch Pro the "bottom" button is B. Prompts on screen change to your controller's buttons as soon as you use it, and go back to keys when you touch the mouse or keyboard.
- Left stick move (in a car: steer) · right stick look · `L3` or `LB/L1/L` run (nitro in a car, descend in the helicopter)
- `RT/R2/ZR` fire / pick up / punch (in a car: accelerate) · `LT/L2/ZL` throw / grab (in a car: brake and reverse)
- Bottom button (`A` / `✕` / `B`) jump, handbrake, climb · top button (`Y` / `△` / `X`) interact, get in and out (the `E` key)
- Right button (`B` / `○` / `A`) use the held item, spray the fire hose · left button (`X` / `□` / `Y`) rob the till, change the radio station
- `RB/R1/R` hostage (on foot), tow hook (in a car) · `View/Share/−` bag it · `R3` horn
- D-pad: up phone, down map, left bag, right vehicle job
- `Menu/Options/+` pause and resume (and starts the game from the title screen)
- In menus, the phone and shops, one button is highlighted, like on a console: the d-pad or left stick moves the highlight, A selects and B goes back (on a Switch Pro that's A on the right and B on the bottom, as printed; on Xbox and PlayStation it's the bottom button to select and the right one to go back), the right stick scrolls long lists (and left/right moves sliders). At the pool table the left stick aims the cue (d-pad for fine aim) and you hold the bottom button or right trigger to build power and let go to shoot.
- The controller rumbles on crashes and explosions (where the browser supports it).
- The title and pause screens show which controllers the browser can see. Every connected controller is read, so a "virtual" one added by Windows, Steam or a remapping tool can't get in the way, and a Switch Pro that the browser reports with a non-standard layout still works (d-pad included).

**Going ashore:** while the ship is moored there's an opening in the rail amidships on the side facing the quay. Walk down the gangway (or just jump off) and you're ashore; walk back up the gangway to come aboard. Sail away by ringing up the engines at the helm; to moor again, bring her back alongside the quay, slowly and roughly parallel, and ring STOP.

**Getting below:** the doorways in the middle of the superstructure, on both sides of the main deck.

**Getting to the bridge:** from the flight deck at the stern, walk forward along either side of the hangar, climb the stairs amidships to the 01 level, go forward between the funnels and the boats, and through the door at the back of the bridge.

## What's in it

- **Ocean:** Gerstner waves in a shader, with a matching CPU wave function so the ship heaves, pitches and rolls on the waves you see. Bow wave, Kelvin wake and churned water astern scale with speed.
- **The ship:** a procedural destroyer: lofted hull with hull number, walkable main deck, 5-inch gun, VLS, superstructure with stairs, two funnels, boats, CIWS, hangar and flight deck, a mast with spinning radar, and an enclosed bridge with consoles, wheel and engine telegraph. She heels into turns.
- **Interior:** doorways amidships on both sides open into a cross passage, with the crew mess forward (tables, galley, mugs and rations) and the storeroom aft (shelving racks and lockers stocked with ammo boxes, toolboxes, medkits, extinguishers, jerry cans, life jackets, rope, spare shells and more). Everything can be picked up, carried, re-shelved or thrown.
- **Your hand:** a robotic arm (you lost your own arm in the Verian Central bombing that killed Wren); whatever you pick up is shown clamped in its gripper.
- **Gunnery:** man the 5-inch gun or either CIWS; rounds fly in world space with gravity and the ship's own speed, splash into the sea and blow up target rafts.
- **Naval air station:** a quay with bollards and lamp posts, an apron with three hangars, a control tower, container stacks and fuel tanks, a 1.3 km runway with a parallel taxiway, and a windsock. It has its own physics world, so you can walk around it while the ship sails off.
- **Fighter jets:** procedural twin-tail navy fighters with a physical-ish flight model (thrust, drag, lift from angle of attack, stalls), taxiing, take-off, landing (or crashing), afterburners, a nose cannon that can hit the target rafts, flight HUD with flight-path marker and STALL / PULL UP warnings. Wrecked jets are replaced after a while.
- **Casualties:** sailors hit hard (a thrown item, cannon fire from a jet) are killed and go ragdoll. Blood sprays, pools under the body and smears when you drag it. Mops are in the stores, by the mess and on the flight deck. Loose bodies and items feel the ship's roll at sea.
- **Settings** (pause screen): townspeople respawn, chase music, crew respawn and crew size on the destroyer, blood on/off, bodies stay or get cleared, mouse sensitivity, invert jet pitch. Saved in your browser.
- **Crew:** sailors in working uniforms, cranials and dixie cups wander the main deck and stop to look at you when you walk up to them.
- **Health:** fall damage and going overboard hurt; health regenerates; dying sends you to the hospital in Verian City.

Physics runs in the ship's own frame (the ship is static there) and the whole frame is moved on the ocean for rendering, so walking on a rolling deck is solid.

## Admin panel (testing)

On the title screen, hold **Alt** and click the very top-left corner of the screen to unlock admin commands (remembered in this browser). In game, press **F8** (or **`**) to open the panel; **Esc** closes it.

Tabs: **Player** (god mode, health/armour, money, wanted 0–20, police off, infinite ammo, debug overlay), **Teleport** (landmarks, homes, the new areas, where you're looking, coordinates), **Items** (any weapon or item, max ammo), **Vehicles** (spawn any car, bike, helicopter, police car or El Monstruo; repair, max upgrades, bulletproof, clear/freeze traffic), **World** (time, clock speed, weather, game speed), **Story** (start/pass/fail missions, key items, force the Act II areas open), **Activities** (races, jobs, packages, ramps, bounty hunters, roadblocks, business raids and tills, stock market crashes and booms) and **Save data** (wipe individual saves, or hide the admin panel again).
